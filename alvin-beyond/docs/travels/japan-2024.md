# Japan · Tokyo to Hokkaido and Tōhoku, 2024

<span class="journal-meta">May 2024 · Tokyo, Lake Tōya, Hakodate, Morioka, Sendai</span>

<div class="photo-placeholder">Feature image — Tokyo / Hokkaido journey</div>

A journey combining SusHi Tech Tokyo with rail travel north into Hokkaido and Tōhoku, including the **5 km ANA Lake Toya Marathon event**.

## Route highlights

- **Tokyo:** SusHi Tech Tokyo 2024.
- **Hokkaido:** Shinkansen to Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto, onward to the Lake Tōya area.
- **Lake Tōya:** Participation in the 5 km running event.
- **Tōhoku:** Hakodate, Morioka and Sendai segments before returning towards Tokyo.
