# About

I'm Alvin, originally from Taiwan, with much of my life spent moving between Asia, Europe and North America.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to live, study and work in different places, with plenty of independent journeys in between. Some trips have taken me to major cities and international events; others have involved quieter excursions, unfamiliar train routes, local restaurants and places I simply wanted to see for myself.

Outside of travelling, I enjoy running, cycling, skiing, sailing and exploring different sports. I'm also keen on football, Formula 1, live music and trying local food wherever I happen to be.

This site brings together a selection of those experiences, from sporting events and concerts to everyday discoveries, photographs and journeys worth remembering.

It's an ongoing personal archive, rather than a comprehensive record of everywhere I've been.
